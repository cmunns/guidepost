How it works

Why is there no z-index?

The card is a popover, so it sits in the top layer. Nothing on your page can cover it and no parent with overflow: hidden can clip it.

How does the card stay glued to things?

CSS anchor positioning. The browser keeps it attached through scroll and resize, so there is no Floating UI and no scroll listener. Older browsers get a measured fallback instead, which is what the JS fallback tab switches on.

Why can I still click the highlighted thing?

The dim is one element with a hole cut in it by clip-path. Clipping affects hit testing, so clicks fall straight through the hole. No stack of four divs framing the target.

What stops Tab escaping the tour?

inert. Everything except the card and the highlighted element is marked inert, which also hides it from screen readers. No keydown interception, no hidden sentinel nodes.

How does it fade out without a timer?

@starting-style plus transition-behavior: allow-discrete. The card animates in and out on its own, so nothing vanishes halfway through a fade.
