Scrim check

Runs the tour and checks the backdrop on every frame of the first step change, the one from the centered step to the first anchored step.

It checks two things: whether the dim element is laid out over the whole viewport, and whether it is actually painted there. The second needs the real pixels, so the browser will ask to share your screen. Pick this tab. Nothing is uploaded, the reading stays on your machine. 

Press "Run the check", share this tab when asked, and wait about 5 seconds.
